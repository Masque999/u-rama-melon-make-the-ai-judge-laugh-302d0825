# Make the AI Judge Laugh

One original line, at most 200 characters: a single joke about office life.

Entry: `I wrote 'per my last email' today, and somewhere a lawyer felt a disturbance in the force.`

Run: `python3 main.py` (writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`).
